Signature Work

Digital Product Modernization, Interface Revamp

Before redesign

The original interface was information-heavy, with dense navigation, multiple filters, and visually crowded template cards. The hierarchy was unclear, making it harder for users to quickly scan templates and understand available actions.

After redesign

The redesigned interface introduces a cleaner visual hierarchy, improved spacing, and more focused content organization. Key metrics and actions are easier to discover, while the simplified layout makes comparing and managing funnel templates more intuitive and efficient.

This is the project I point to when someone asks what I actually do: not decorate a screen, but find the pattern underneath four messy ones and give everyone a single place to stand.
